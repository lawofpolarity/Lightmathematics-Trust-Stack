# SCITT Comparator

SCITT is a required comparator for LM ledger and receipt claims.

## Comparator question

Given identical signed statements, policies, dependency information, and
runtime capabilities, can SCITT plus a conventional dependency/policy engine
produce the same decisions, explanations, invalidations, and receipts as LM?

If yes, classify the result as comparator parity.

If no, isolate the smallest residual difference and test it prospectively.

## Claims LM must not make merely from receipt existence

- novelty of signed claim registration;
- novelty of immutable/transparency receipts;
- novelty of policy-applied registration;
- novelty of append-only evidence history.
